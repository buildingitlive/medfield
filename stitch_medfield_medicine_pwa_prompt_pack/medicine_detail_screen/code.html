Product Information

Amoxicillin is a penicillin antibiotic used to treat various bacterial infections, including respiratory tract infections, urinary tract infections, and skin infections. It works by stopping the growth of bacteria.
Take this medication by mouth with or without food as directed by your doctor, usually every 8 or 12 hours. The dosage is based on your medical condition and response to treatment.
Common side effects may include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, or rash. If any of these effects last or get worse, tell your doctor or pharmacist promptly.
